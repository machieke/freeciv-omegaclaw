# FDAS PR38 action-family yield result

## Result

The preregistered independent-yield cohort passed every mechanical and frozen
progression gate. A fresh action-stratified calibration discovery design may
now be implemented. PR38 itself remains excluded from model fitting and later
confirmation.

All four seeds completed turn 160 from identical clean commit
`53b144440bde7a6c8a78a2caa13ee4a5241454c5`. The 78,700-event cohort has zero
validation errors, warnings, rejected actions, or infrastructure failures.
All stores are independent, current, non-quarantined, outcome-free,
selected-only, explicitly censored for alternatives, and non-authorizing.

## Frozen-gate outcome

The surface produced:

- 191 opportunity sets and 422 exact unambiguous candidates;
- 109 multi-candidate and 15 mixed move/fortify sets;
- 165 no-in-scope-selection sets and 396 censored alternatives;
- 26 selected actions: nine moves and 17 fortifications;
- 25 observed delayed outcomes: 19 positive and six negative;
- 23 selected actor/context signatures and 20 feature signatures;
- two selected and observed move `(game, actor, stratum)` lineages; and
- 17 selected and observed fortification lineages.

The observed-move lineage count equals, rather than exceeds, the frozen
minimum. A discovery design must therefore retain action-stratum separation,
cluster route steps by durable lifecycle lineage, expose wide uncertainty,
and abstain rather than borrow unsupported precision from fortification.

## What this permits

PR38 permits implementation and preregistration of a fresh discovery cohort
for an artifact that:

- predicts realized 32-turn selected-actor city-defense persistence;
- calibrates move and fortify action strata separately;
- conditions or backs off by declared lifecycle state;
- groups repeated route steps by game/actor/target lifecycle;
- reports support, effective lineage count, and confidence interval;
- labels no nonselected candidate; and
- grants no policy or readout authority.

It does not establish a calibrated model, candidate ranking, counterfactual
effect for censored alternatives, decision-safe readout, gameplay impact,
score improvement, or win rate.
